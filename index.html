About the studio

Designing with
intention since 2008

Founded in Pretori and now operating across three continents, ARKEN Studio is driven by a conviction that great architecture elevates everyday life. Every project begins with deep listening — to the land, the client, and the culture of place.

Our multidisciplinary team of 34 architects, interior designers, and structural engineers collaborates obsessively to deliver spaces that are both rigorously functional and quietly beautiful.

Our services

From concept sketches to final handover, we guide every stage of your project.

01

Residential Architecture

Private homes and multi-unit residences designed around how you actually live — light, airflow, and material honesty at every turn.

02

Commercial Spaces

Offices, retail, and hospitality environments that perform under pressure and leave a lasting impression on every visitor.

03

Interior Design

Full interior architecture services: spatial planning, material palettes, bespoke joinery, and art curation, all under one roof.

04

Sustainable Design

Passive-house principles, biophilic design, and low-carbon material sourcing baked into every project from day one.

05

Urban Planning

Master-plan strategies and public-realm design for municipalities and developers reshaping the urban fabric.

06

Project Management

End-to-end construction administration and contractor coordination so your vision is faithfully realised on site, on time, on budget.
